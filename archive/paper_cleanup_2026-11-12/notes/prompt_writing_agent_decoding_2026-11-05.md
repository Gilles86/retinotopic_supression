Update for the retsupp paper plan. Read `notes/paper_plan_2026-11.md` and `notes/results_overview_2026-11.md`.

We are adding a secondary, gain-free test. Stimulus decoding with a pRF model that has no gain terms shows less bar drive at the HP location than at LP locations in early visual cortex (V1-V3, n = 25, p = .002 and .003), the same direction as dS. These May results used the old inputs, were one-sided and uncorrected, and have n below 30 for reasons not yet known, so do not cite the numbers.

Plan for it as converging evidence: pre-specified in Phase B, rerun on the final inputs in Phase C (C6), shown as Fig. 5e. Leave placeholders for the numbers, and keep the primary claim on dS against the HP-rotation null.

Voxel selection is now cross-run reliability only. HRF per subject × ROI is under test. All gain numbers remain provisional until Phase C.
