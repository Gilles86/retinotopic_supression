**Figure. The response at the HP location, per item type.** Deconvolved response (nideconv) of voxels whose pRF centre
lies within 2° of an item location, mean over 3.2-6.4 s after search onset, all nine ROIs pooled weighted by voxel
count; per subject the response when the location is HP relative to when it is an LP location, in %. Dots are subjects
(triangles beyond the axis), points and bars the mean and 95% CI (t), n = 30. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests against 0:
target -4.1% [-9.8, +1.6], p = 0.16; distractor -7.2% [-14.4, +0.0], p = 0.0087; neutral -3.0% [-11.2, +5.2], p = 0.13. The bracket compares the distractor with the mean of target and neutral item (-3.6%, p = 0.08).
