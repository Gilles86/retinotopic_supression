Panels a and b as in Fig. 7: a, the gain at the target on target-repeat trials and at the distractor on distractor-repeat trials, relative to the area's typical gain in the main model's fits, × 100%; b, the change in each gain per SD of log RT. Points and bars, posterior mean and 95% HDI of the mean over all nine areas (left, larger) and of each pathway mean; solid when the posterior probability of the sign shown exceeds .975; stars, * > .975, ** > .995, *** > .9995.
