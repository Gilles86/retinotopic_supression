The display is rotated so that the HP location is at the top (purple dashed ring); small circles, the eight item positions on the 4° ring (dotted; stronger: the four possible target and distractor locations); grey circle, the 3.17° aperture of the mapping bar; +, fixation. Left three maps, the sustained field at HP minus at the low-probability (LP) locations: only the HP-specific part of the sustained field, the area's posterior mean HP − LP difference (dS, the value panel c plots, printed in bold next to HP) times the field's Gaussian at HP (width: the area's median full width at half maximum among fits whose sustained gain is reliably different from zero, |gain| > 2 SE; Supplementary Fig., field widths); thin purple line, the half−maximum (FWHM) contour of that field. One scale for the three areas, white (no difference) to deep purple at the deepest area's HP − LP (−6.0%; no headroom, so that area saturates). Right three maps, the transient fields of the target (orange, around the orange diamond, at an LP location, here opposite HP) and of the distractor (green, around the green dot, drawn at an LP location next to HP) in one map; values, the target gain of panel b and the distractor gain averaged over its locations, as in panel b (the transient HP − LP difference is in panel d); both on one scale, 0 to the largest value, so their colours compare directly.
