Each session or block alone holds a half or a quarter of the data, and the per-split tests are descriptive: a difference between a significant and a non-significant split is not itself significant. In session 2 the two neighbours of HP were HP locations in session 1, so carry-over cannot be strictly excluded for the neighbour effect; a location that was HP before does not respond less than one that was never HP (`richter_followup_2026-11-11.md`, A2, p = .54).
