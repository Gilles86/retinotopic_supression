**Supplementary Table. How well the three pRF models fit the same voxels.** For each participant and area, the in-sample R² of the joint zero-sum gain-field fit with Gaussian, DoG and DN pRFs on the same voxels (45,405 voxels in 241 participant × area fits), all runs (about 3,100 time points per voxel). R² gain: the median over voxels of the R² with DoG or DN pRFs minus that with Gaussian pRFs; over chance: that gain divided by what the extra per-voxel parameters (2 for DoG, 4 for DN) add by chance in a nested least-squares fit, k (1 − R²) / n. Per area, the median over participants with a bootstrapped 95% CI; pathways and all areas, per participant the mean over their areas. Because the BOLD noise is autocorrelated, the effective number of time points is smaller than n, so "over chance" overstates the evidence by the same factor in every area: compare areas with it, rather than reading it as a test. The pRF-only fits (no gain fields) give the same values to within about 0.001.
