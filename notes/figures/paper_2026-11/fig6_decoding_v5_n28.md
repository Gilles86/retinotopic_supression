Per subject and run, the BOLD of V1–V3 was simulated from the subject's joint fit (pRFs fitted together with the gain fields on all runs: sustained HP and LP gains and field width, transient distractor and target gains, search items, one HRF per subject and ROI) plus the real run's residuals under that fit, phase-randomised (one random phase per frequency for all voxels, so the noise keeps each voxel's spectrum and the covariance between voxels but nothing locked to the stimulus), and decoded exactly as the data (the same fold pRFs without gain terms, which never saw the run, and the same noise model and readouts), on the decoder's voxels that are also in the joint fit; a paired simulation with the five gains at 0 and the same noise was decoded alike, and the prediction is the difference (model − gains at 0) per subject, averaged like the data (n = 28).
