**Figure S. Gain at the target and the distractor against pRF size.** Median target and distractor gain per ROI (as in Fig. 5a) against the ROI's pRF size (median over subjects of the voxel-weighted mean pRF σ at 1–2.5° eccentricity, log scale). Lines: slope per doubling of pRF size, the median of the per-subject OLS slopes over the nine ROIs, drawn through the centre of the points; target +0.24 per doubling (95% CI [+0.15, +0.27], 90% of subjects positive, p = 8e-06, q = 6.4e-05); distractor +0.06 per doubling (95% CI [+0.03, +0.11], 77% of subjects positive, p = 0.005, q = 0.01) (Wilcoxon signed-rank test of the per-subject slopes, Benjamini-Hochberg over the eight parameters tested). The slope may partly reflect a trade-off in the fits: within subjects, the target gain correlates with the fitted weight of the items relative to the bar (ρ = .25, p = .011) and negatively with the size of the gain field (ρ = −.22, p = .049), and the model-free enhancement at the item locations (Fig. 3) rises much less up the hierarchy.
