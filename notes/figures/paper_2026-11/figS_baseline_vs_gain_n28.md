**Figure S. Gain or baseline shift at the HP location.** n = 28; current final fits (DoG pRFs and one HRF per participant × area fitted jointly; gain fields fitted with these pRFs frozen). **a**, What the two accounts predict for the response to the mapping bar where it crosses the HP location: a gain scales the response; an additive offset shifts it. Each run's time series is expressed as percent signal change of its own mean, and the HP location is constant within a run, so a sustained offset at HP is removed with the run mean and cannot be estimated (the additive sustained regressor keeps 0.0 of its sum of squares after run-mean removal in every participant × area). **b**, Slope of the response to the bar where it crosses the HP location, relative to the other locations: for each participant, area and item location (voxels with a pRF centre within 2° of the location, as in Fig. 3), the location's mean BOLD regressed on the bar-only pRF prediction with one slope per relation to the run's HP location (HP, next to HP, opposite HP), a free intercept per run and a 10-bin response (0–16 s) per item class at search onsets; 100 × (slope at HP / mean slope at the LP locations − 1); per participant the median over the areas of a group; median over participants and bootstrapped 95% CI (10,000 resamples); Wilcoxon signed-rank against 0, * p < .05. All areas −2.1% [−4.6, −0.7], 20 of 28 participants below 0, p = .012; at HP vs next to HP −3.5% [−5.2, −0.9], 21/28, p = .0011; next vs opposite −0.5%, p = .78. Early −3.4%, p = .10; dorsal −2.2%, p = .55; occipitotemporal −2.5%, p = .063 (pale: p ≥ .05). **c**, A trial-locked additive response at the HP location, added to each participant's joint gain-field fit: a response at every search onset (left) or fixation onset (right), convolved with the area's HRF, with an amplitude per voxel proportional to the overlap of its pRF with the sustained field at HP minus at the other three locations, plus a trial response common to all four locations; amplitudes estimated on all runs but the third of each session and scored on the third (held-out ΔR² relative to the joint fit, × 10⁻⁴; dots, participants, median over areas; point and bar, median and bootstrapped 95% CI). Locked to search: HP-specific amplitude +1.5% signal change at full overlap [+0.0, +2.9], 19 of 28 positive, p = .010 (positive: no suppression), held-out better in 16 of 28 participants, p = .41. Locked to fixation: HP-specific amplitude −0.7% [−1.0, +0.9], p = .85; the held-out gain (24 of 28, p = 1 × 10⁻⁴) comes from the response common to all locations. **d**, Recovery: data simulated from each participant's fitted pRFs with phase-randomised residuals (one random phase spectrum per run, shared by all voxels) and either a sustained gain at HP of the group size (−1.4%), a trial-locked additive response at HP matched to it in signal power at the voxels nearest HP, or a sustained additive offset (then removed with each run's mean, as by the cleaning); 10 simulated datasets of all 28 participants each, fitted with both terms together; detected = group Wilcoxon p < .05 with a negative estimate. Proportion of simulated datasets per outcome.
