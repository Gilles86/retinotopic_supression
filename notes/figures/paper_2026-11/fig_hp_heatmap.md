**Figure. Less response at the HP location, per item type and pathway.** Deconvolved response (nideconv) of voxels
whose pRF centre lies within 2° of an item location, mean over 3.2-6.4 s after search onset, ROIs pooled per
pathway weighted by voxel count; per subject, the response when the location is HP minus when it is an LP location,
in % of the subject's mean LP response over the three item types in that pathway. Cells: mean over subjects (n = 30).
Stars in cells: Wilcoxon signed-rank test against 0, uncorrected. Right: per pathway, a repeated-measures ANOVA
location (HP, LP) × item type (target, distractor, neutral) on the same normalised responses; main effect of
location and the location × item interaction, and the planned contrast distractor vs the mean of target and neutral
item (Wilcoxon). Early: location F(1, 29) = 4.52, p = 0.0421; location × item F(2, 58) = 0.57, p = 0.571; distractor vs others -3.0%, p = 0.339; Dorsal: location F(1, 27) = 0.41, p = 0.527; location × item F(2, 54) = 1.12, p = 0.335; distractor vs others +2.5%, p = 0.955; Occipitotemporal: location F(1, 29) = 0.41, p = 0.526; location × item F(2, 58) = 1.55, p = 0.22; distractor vs others -8.3%, p = 0.0767; All areas: location F(1, 29) = 5.08, p = 0.0319; location × item F(2, 58) = 0.39, p = 0.679; distractor vs others -4.2%, p = 0.382
