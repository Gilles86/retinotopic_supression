**Figure S. Gain at the target and the distractor against pRF size.** Gain-field model fits with DoG pRFs, zero-sum sustained field, pRFs and gain fields fitted jointly (LM). Median target and distractor gain per area (as in Fig. 5b; gains in %) against the area's pRF size (median over subjects of the voxel-weighted mean pRF σ at 1–2.5° eccentricity, log scale). Lines: slope per doubling of pRF size, the median of the per-subject OLS slopes over the nine areas, drawn through the centre of the points; target +32% per doubling (95% CI [+20%, +79%], 82% of participants positive, p = 8.2e−05, q = 0.00057); distractor +6% per doubling (95% CI [−10%, +32%], 64% of participants positive, p = 0.12, q = 0.21) (Wilcoxon signed-rank test of the per-subject slopes, Benjamini-Hochberg over the eight parameters tested). The slope may partly reflect a trade-off in the fits: within subjects (Spearman ρ over the nine areas, Wilcoxon across subjects), the target gain does not track the fitted weight of the items relative to the bar (median ρ = +0.05, p = 0.32) but falls with the width of the transient field (median ρ = −0.64, p = 3.7e−08), and the model-free enhancement at the item locations (Fig. 3) rises much less up the hierarchy.
