**Figure 5. The gain fields: gain at the target and the distractor grows up the visual hierarchy; at the high-probability (HP) location there is less sustained gain.** Gain-field model fits with DoG pRFs (n = 30); a gain is the fractional change in drive at the centre of a field (+0.30: 30% more). Per-ROI panels (b, c, e, f): dots are subjects (triangles, beyond the axis), points and bars the median and bootstrapped 95% CI; stars, Wilcoxon signed-rank test against 0, Benjamini-Hochberg across the nine ROIs, * q < .05, ** q < .01, *** q < .001. Squares under the data (New subjects): leave-one-subject-out test per ROI, the term added with the size estimated from the other 29 subjects (+δ) vs the opposite size (−δ); filled, a majority of subjects predicted better with +δ (one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test, Benjamini-Hochberg q < .05 across ROIs); open, not significant. The display icon right of each panel marks the item or location the gain applies to. a, Median target and distractor gain per ROI (as in b, c) against the ROI's pRF size (median over subjects of the voxel-weighted mean pRF σ at 1–2.5° eccentricity, log scale). Lines: slope per doubling of pRF size, the median of the per-subject OLS slopes over the nine ROIs, drawn through the centre of the points; target +0.24 per doubling (95% CI [+0.15, +0.27], 90% of subjects positive, p = 8e-06, q = 6.4e-05); distractor +0.06 per doubling (95% CI [+0.03, +0.11], 77% of subjects positive, p = 0.005, q = 0.01) (Wilcoxon signed-rank test of the per-subject slopes, Benjamini-Hochberg over the eight parameters tested). b, Gain at the target location while the search display is on (all terms fitted jointly). c, Gain at the distractor, averaged over its locations (joint fit); for the New-subjects test, the distractor gain added on top of the target term. d, The sustained gain field over the search display, drawn to scale (red shading): one Gaussian per item (white discs, 4° eccentricity, neighbouring items 5.7° apart; grey circle, the 3.17° aperture of the bar stimulus), with less gain at the HP than at the low-probability (LP) locations (the HP − LP difference exaggerated for display). Dashed circle: the full width at half maximum (FWHM = 2.355 σ) of the fitted sustained field, the median over all subjects and ROIs (4.8°; per-ROI medians: V1 5.6°; V2 6.4°; V3 5.6°; V3AB 4.6°; IPS 6.6°; hV4 3.9°; VO 4.3°; LO 4.6°; TO 3.7°; 12% of the fits at a bound of σ, 0.5° or 8°). The field reaches beyond the item and overlaps those of its neighbours. e, dS = g_HP − g_LP, the sustained gain at the HP location minus that at the LP locations, each term estimated alone on the item model with sustained gain (conditional estimate). f, dD = g_HP_dyn − g_LP_dyn, the gain the distractor draws at the HP location minus at an LP location, estimated alone as in e. When dS and dD are fitted together with all other terms (supplementary figure and parameter table), no ROI survives correction: dD stays at −7 to −10% in V3AB, IPS, hV4 and VO (q ≈ .09–.15), dS is −4 to −5% in V1–V3 (q ≥ .07). The measured responses at the item locations (Fig. 4) show less response at HP for every item type, significant in the early areas, without a distractor-specific difference. DN pRFs, Bayes factors and all other terms are in the parameter table. Provisional: current inputs.
