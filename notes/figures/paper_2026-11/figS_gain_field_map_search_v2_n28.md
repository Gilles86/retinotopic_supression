**Supplementary Figure. The fitted gain field during a search display: distractor at the HP location vs at an LP location.** The fitted parametric field, not data, as in Fig. 5a: for each area (V3, hV4, TO), the median over participants (n = 28) of each participant's fitted parameters, put through the model's field equations; value, the change in gain on the stimulus drive in % (differences between gains in percentage points, written as %). Each map is the sum of the sustained field and the transient target and distractor fields, as in the model's forward pass, during a search display with the target (orange diamond) opposite the HP location and the distractor (blue dot) at HP (top row) or at the LP location next to HP (bottom row); only the distractor's location differs between the two rows. Sustained field: median joint−fit gain at the LP locations; at HP, that plus the area's median HP − LP difference estimated alone (dS), exactly as in Fig. 5a. Distractor gain: at an LP location the median joint−fit LP distractor gain; at HP, that plus the median joint−fit transient HP − LP difference (dD); target gain, the median joint−fit target gain. Transient width set for display to FWHM 3.2° in every area, not interpreted. The transient HP − LP difference is not reliable in any area, jointly fitted or estimated alone (Fig. 5d legend), so the transient part of the HP − LP difference drawn here is not an effect. The line under each column gives the gain at the distractor with the distractor at HP minus with the distractor at LP: the sustained plus the transient HP − LP difference. Colour: magma, one scale per area shared by the two rows (colour bar under each column); teal line and tick, no change. HP at the top (red dashed ring); small circles, the eight item positions on the 4° ring; grey circle, the 3.17° bar aperture. Values: V3: at the distractor +7.8% with the distractor at HP, −0.6% at LP (HP − LP +8.4%; sustained HP − LP, dS estimated alone, −3.0%; transient HP − LP, joint dD, +11%); at the target +30%; hV4: at the distractor +24% with the distractor at HP, +15% at LP (HP − LP +9.2%; sustained HP − LP, dS estimated alone, −1.5%; transient HP − LP, joint dD, +11%); at the target +50%; TO: at the distractor −28% with the distractor at HP, −27% at LP (HP − LP −1.9%; sustained HP − LP, dS estimated alone, −1.9%; transient HP − LP, joint dD, 0%); at the target −14%.
