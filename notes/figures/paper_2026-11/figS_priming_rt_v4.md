**Supplementary Figure figS_priming_rt_v4. Response times behind the intertrial priming of Fig. 1e.** Response time when the target (a) or the distractor (b) appears at the same location as on the previous trial (repeat) or at another location (switch); distractor repeats are compared within HP and within LP trials and averaged. Icons: the four possible locations; filled, the item on this trial, hollow, on the previous trial. n = 30, correct trials. The brackets give the median within-subject difference and the Wilcoxon signed-rank test (** p < .01, *** p < .001); the differences themselves are in Fig. 1e. Dots are subjects, triangles subjects beyond the axis limits; large points and bars are the mean ± within-subject SEM (Cousineau-Morey).
