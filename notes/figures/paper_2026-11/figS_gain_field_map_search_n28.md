**Supplementary Figure S. The fitted gain field during a search display: distractor at the HP location vs at an LP location.** The fitted parametric field, not data, as in Fig. 5a: for each area (V3, hV4, TO), the median over participants (n = 28) of each participant's joint-fit parameter, put through the model's field equations; value, the fractional change in gain on the stimulus drive. Each map is the sum of the sustained field and the transient target and distractor fields of Fig. 5a, as in the model's forward pass, during a search display with the target (orange diamond) opposite the HP location and the distractor (blue dot) at HP (top row) or at the LP location next to HP (bottom row); only the distractor's location differs between the two rows. Sustained field as in Fig. 5a (median LP gain; HP gain = median LP gain + median joint dS). Distractor gain: at an LP location the median g_LP_dyn, at HP that plus the median joint transient HP − LP difference (dD), the same rule as for the sustained field; target gain, the median g_T_dyn. Transient width set for display to FWHM 3.2° in every area, not interpreted. The joint transient HP − LP difference is not reliable in any area (Fig. 5d legend: smallest q = 0.29), so the transient part of the HP − LP difference drawn here is not an effect. The line under each column gives the gain at the distractor with the distractor at HP minus with the distractor at LP: the sustained HP − LP difference plus the transient HP − LP difference. Colour: magma, one scale per area shared by the two rows (colour bar under each column); teal line and tick, no change. HP at the top (red dashed ring); small circles, the eight item positions on the 4° ring; grey circle, the 3.17° bar aperture. Values: V3: at the distractor +0.02 with the distractor at HP, −0.01 at LP (HP − LP +0.03; sustained HP − LP −0.09, transient HP − LP +0.11); at the target +0.30; hV4: at the distractor +0.20 with the distractor at HP, +0.15 at LP (HP − LP +0.05; sustained HP − LP −0.06, transient HP − LP +0.11); at the target +0.50; TO: at the distractor −0.29 with the distractor at HP, −0.27 at LP (HP − LP −0.02; sustained HP − LP −0.02, transient HP − LP 0.00); at the target −0.14.
