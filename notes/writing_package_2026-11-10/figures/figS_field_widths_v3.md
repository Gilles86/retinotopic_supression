**Figure S. Width of the gain fields.** Full width at half maximum (FWHM = 2.355 σ) per subject and area of the sustained field (red) and of the transient field shared by the target and the distractor gain (grey), from the joint gain-field fits with DoG pRFs (m7, final fits; n = 30; log axis). Dots are subjects; points and bars, the median and its bootstrapped 95% CI over all fits, including those at a bound. Numbers above and below a series: fits at the upper (σ = 8°, FWHM 18.8°) or lower (σ = 0.5°, FWHM 1.2°) bound of σ, not drawn as dots. Dotted line: the spacing of neighbouring items (5.7°). Sustained: 14% of all fits at a bound (per area V1 27%, V2 13%, V3 7%, V3AB 14%, IPS 11%, hV4 17%, VO 13%, LO 10%, TO 10%); median FWHM [interquartile range over subjects] V1 5.3° [3.5–18.7]; V2 5.0° [3.5–9.3]; V3 5.0° [2.8–6.9]; V3AB 3.4° [2.7–4.6]; IPS 3.1° [2.5–5.5]; hV4 2.9° [2.6–7.9]; VO 2.6° [2.4–3.8]; LO 3.2° [2.7–6.7]; TO 2.5° [2.3–2.8]. Transient: often at a bound (per area V1 43%, V2 50%, V3 50%, V3AB 62%, IPS 52%, hV4 63%, VO 35%, LO 47%, TO 53%; IPS mostly at the upper bound), so its width is poorly constrained and trades off with the transient gain amplitude; median FWHM [IQR] V1 14.2° [6.5–18.8]; V2 11.2° [4.3–18.8]; V3 6.0° [1.2–16.9]; V3AB 1.3° [1.2–7.6]; IPS 12.3° [4.7–18.8]; hV4 1.2° [1.2–4.6]; VO 5.0° [1.2–9.5]; LO 5.0° [1.8–18.1]; TO 1.7° [1.2–4.5]. The sustained width is the well-determined one. At half maximum the sustained field (median FWHM above) stays within the spacing of neighbouring items in most ROIs (early areas ≈ 5°, others ≈ 3°).
