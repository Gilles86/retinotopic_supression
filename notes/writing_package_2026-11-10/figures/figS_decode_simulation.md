**Figure S. The decoder recovers a gain field placed at the HP location only.** Simulation: for every subject and run, BOLD was generated from the DoG fold pRFs (canonical HRF) with a sustained gain field at the HP item only (FWHM 4.8°, amplitude 0, −0.025 or −0.075), plus phase-randomised real residuals, and decoded exactly as in Fig. 6 (unconstrained and outline decoders). Per subject, ROI and amplitude, the decoded bar intensity per quadrant (HP, the two quadrants next to HP, opposite) is expressed in % of the mean of the four quadrants and averaged over runs; the effect of the field is the paired difference from the amplitude-0 simulation with the same noise draw; pooled = mean over V1–V3. The decoder recovers about 85% of the true quadrant pattern of the simulated stimulus (unconstrained decoder, V1–V3 pooled: HP − next −0.88% decoded vs −1.03% true at amplitude −0.025; −2.67% vs −3.12% at −0.075). An HP-only field lowers HP and leaves the neighbouring quadrants below the opposite one (next − opposite −0.24% at −0.025), the pattern a field centred on HP must produce; the observed decoded bar (Fig. 6e) shows HP below its neighbours (−0.85% vs +0.46%) but next to HP not below opposite (+0.46% vs −0.08%, n.s.). An HP gain of −0.025, between the fitted sustained HP − LP gain estimated alone (−0.012) and jointly (−0.031), produces a decoded HP − next difference of the same order as observed (−0.88% simulated, −1.31% observed). n = 30; all simulated effects p < .001 (Wilcoxon signed-rank against 0).
