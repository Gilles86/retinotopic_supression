**Figure 7. The gain fields fluctuate with trial history and with response time.** Trial-by-trial modulation of the gain fields, estimated on top of each subject's final joint gain-field fit (m7: DoG pRFs, one HRF per subject × area, each area's signal voxels). Per subject and area, the target, distractor and global gains of every trial were written as a gain plus modulators (z-scored log RT; target at the previous target's location, distractor at the previous distractor's location; target at the previous distractor's location), all centred within gain, and estimated directly on the BOLD time series of all the area's voxels (weighted least squares on the linear phasic term, other parameters fixed). The uncertainty of every subject × area estimate is its leave-one-run-out jackknife SE (12 runs; it agrees with the odd-versus-even-run difference, and includes noise shared across voxels, which a split over voxels misses by a factor of about six). Hierarchical Bayesian model per effect: estimate ~ Normal(θ, √(SE² + τ²)), θ = group mean + area deviation + subject deviation, the three group means (early V1–V3; dorsal V3AB, IPS; occipitotemporal hV4, LO, TO, VO) as fixed effects with Normal(0, 2·SD) priors, HalfNormal SDs (PyMC with nutpie, 4 × 2000 draws; 1 divergences over all 27 fits, max R̂ 1.01, min bulk ESS 880; 95% posterior predictive intervals cover 95–99% of the estimates and every area mean). Points and bars: posterior mean and 95% HDI of the mean over all nine areas (left, larger) and of each group's mean; solid when the posterior probability of the sign shown exceeds .975, pale otherwise. Per-area estimates are in the table below. Stars give the posterior probability of the sign shown: * > .975, ** > .995, *** > .9995. n = 28 subjects (230 subject × area estimates; pairs with fewer than 10 signal voxels are left out). a, History: the gain at the target on target-repeat trials and at the distractor on distractor-repeat trials, relative to the area's typical gain (the median over subjects of |g_T| and of |(g_HP + g_LP)/2| in the m7 fits; the median of the absolute value because the signed median is close to 0 in some areas), × 100%.
