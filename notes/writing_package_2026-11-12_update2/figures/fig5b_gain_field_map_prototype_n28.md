**Figure 5b (prototype). The fitted gain field over the visual field.** The joint gain-field fit's parametric field, drawn from the group medians of its parameters (n = 28 participants; the median of each parameter taken separately over participants, per area), not data. The display is rotated so that the high-probability (HP) distractor location is at the top (red dashed ring); the other three possible target and distractor locations are at the left, bottom (opposite HP) and right; dotted circle, the 4° ring of item locations; solid circle, the 3.17° aperture of the mapping bar; +, fixation. Colour: the multiplicative gain on the stimulus drive at each point of the visual field (1, white, no change; below 1, purple, suppression; above 1, orange, enhancement), one scale for all panels. Top row, the sustained field between search displays: a Gaussian gain field (width σ = the fitted sustained width) at each of the four locations, with the gain at HP and the common gain at the other three fitted separately. Bottom row, during a search display with the distractor at HP (blue dot) and the target at the location opposite HP (orange diamond): the sustained field plus the transient fields at the distractor and at the target (one shared transient width). Group medians: V1: gain at HP −0.043, at the other three +0.028, sustained width 2.21°; transient gain at the distractor at HP +0.038, at the target −0.018, width 4.02° (sustained field at the HP item 0.96, opposite 1.03; during the display 0.99 and 1.02); V3: gain at HP −0.138, at the other three −0.004, sustained width 1.94°; transient gain at the distractor at HP +0.091, at the target +0.300, width 2.32° (sustained field at the HP item 0.86, opposite 1.00; during the display 0.95 and 1.30); hV4: gain at HP −0.160, at the other three −0.058, sustained width 1.29°; transient gain at the distractor at HP +0.304, at the target +0.558, width 0.50° (sustained field at the HP item 0.84, opposite 0.94; during the display 1.14 and 1.50); TO: gain at HP −0.476, at the other three −0.354, sustained width 1.07°; transient gain at the distractor at HP +0.087, at the target +0.217, width 0.73° (sustained field at the HP item 0.52, opposite 0.65; during the display 0.61 and 0.86). The transient width is often at a bound of the fit (0.5° or 8°) in single participants, so its median is approximate.
