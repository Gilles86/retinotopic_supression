**Figure S. Gain at the target and the distractor against pRF size.** Median target and distractor gain per ROI (as in Fig. 5a) against the ROI's pRF size (median over subjects of the voxel-weighted mean pRF σ at 1–2.5° eccentricity, log scale). Lines: slope per doubling of pRF size, the median of the per-subject OLS slopes over the nine ROIs, drawn through the centre of the points; target +0.33 per doubling (95% CI [+0.24, +0.67], 82% of subjects positive, p = 0.00042, q = 0.0017); distractor +0.11 per doubling (95% CI [-0.10, +0.29], 61% of subjects positive, p = 0.16, q = 0.33) (Wilcoxon signed-rank test of the per-subject slopes, Benjamini-Hochberg over the eight parameters tested). The slope may partly reflect a trade-off in the fits: within subjects (Spearman ρ over the nine ROIs, Wilcoxon across subjects), the target gain does not track the fitted weight of the items relative to the bar (median ρ = +0.07, p = 0.35) but falls with the width of the transient field (median ρ = −0.62, p = 6.5e−06), and the model-free enhancement at the item locations (Fig. 3) rises much less up the hierarchy.
