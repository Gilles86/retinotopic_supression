**Figure S. The gain terms along the visual hierarchy.** Gain terms per area (joint gain-field fits with DoG pRFs, the pRFs refitted together with the gain fields; n = 28 participants), in % gain. The hierarchy axis is each area's pRF size: the median over participants of the voxel-weighted mean pRF σ at 1–2.5° eccentricity, the axis of the target-gain regression in the main text. Per participant, each term's per-area estimates were regressed on log2 of the area's pRF size, giving the change per doubling of pRF size. Each area is fitted with the stimulus as its input, so an area's gain is the gain accumulated up to that area, not the gain added at that stage. a, Area medians of the target gain, the distractor gain (averaged over its four locations, weighted by how often the distractor appeared there), the sustained gain shared by the four locations (estimated alone) and the sustained gain at HP minus that at the other locations (dS, estimated alone). Each term is divided by its pooled median (median over participants of the median over areas), so a positive slope means the term grows in its own direction and the slopes can be compared across terms; lines, the median per-participant slope. b, Normalised slopes per term, median and bootstrapped 95% CI. In percentage points per doubling: target gain +27.4 percentage points per doubling [+15.3, +68.3], 22/28 positive, p = 0.0022, q = 0.005; distractor gain +11.4 percentage points per doubling [-11.2, +32.1], 17/28 positive, p = 0.16, q = 0.28; shared sustained gain -6.4 percentage points per doubling [-8.0, -5.1], 1/28 positive, p = 2.2e-08, q = 1e-07; dS -0.7 percentage points per doubling [-3.7, +3.3], 12/28 positive, p = 0.61, q = 0.76 (Wilcoxon signed-rank; Benjamini-Hochberg across the nine terms tested). The target slope exceeds the dS slope in % (paired p = 0.018) but not relative to each term's size (p = 0.49). c, Within each area, the five gains re-estimated for the smallest, middle and largest third of its voxels by pRF size (least squares, all other parameters fixed at the fit; areas with fewer than 60 voxels dropped, mostly IPS and VO); dS here is from the joint fit. Median over participants of the median over areas, bootstrapped 95% CI. Slopes on log2 pRF size within areas: target gain +7.3 percentage points per doubling (p = 0.0065), dS +1.9 (p = 0.18); within an area, pRF size and eccentricity go together, so this does not separate the two. d, Full width at half maximum of the sustained field in fits with a reliable sustained gain (|gain|/SE > 2 at HP or at the other locations; leave-one-run-out jackknife SE). Dots, fits; diamonds, area medians, coloured by area as in the main figures; fits kept: V1 8, V2 8, V3 17, V3AB 21, IPS 17, hV4 19, VO 20, LO 19, TO 26. Line, median per-participant slope, -0.80° per doubling [-1.59, -0.13] (28 participants, p = 0.022); at |gain|/SE > 3, -0.04° [-0.32, +0.10] (21 participants, p = 0.43). Dashed, the 5.7° spacing of neighbouring possible target and distractor locations.
