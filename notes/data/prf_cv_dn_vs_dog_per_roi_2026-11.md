# DN vs DoG pRFs per ROI (2026-11-06)

Held-out R² from the leave-one-run-set-out fits (3 folds; `notes/data/prf_cv_roi_summary.tsv`), no refits. Subject value per ROI: the fold mean of the median held-out R² over the ROI's voxels. ΔR² = DN − DoG per subject; median with bootstrapped 95% CI; Wilcoxon signed-rank (two-sided), q: Benjamini-Hochberg across the nine ROIs. "Pooled (median over 9 ROIs)": each subject's median over the nine ROIs. "Pooled (all-ROI voxels)": the summary's `all` row, one median over the voxels of all eleven ROIs together (including SPL1 and FEF), the basis of the earlier "DN > DoG in 30/30". Script: `retsupp/visualize/paper/prf_cv_dn_vs_dog.py`.
