**Figure 3. At the item locations, without a model: more response to the target and the distractor, and, in early areas, least response at the high-probability (HP) location, less next to it than opposite it, for every item.** All panels: deconvolved response (nideconv, Fourier basis) around search onset of voxels whose pRF centre lies within 2° of an item location (inset in a: the display with one such pRF on an item, and the response it gives), areas pooled weighted by voxel count, each subject's pre-onset mean (−4 to 0 s) subtracted; mean over subjects ± SEM (n = 28). Pathways: early, V1–V3; dorsal, V3AB and IPS; occipitotemporal, hV4, VO, LO and TO (in b, c: solid, dashed and dotted lines, in the colour of the item). Grey band: the window tested, 3.2–6.4 s. a, Response at a pRF's item location when the target, the distractor or a neutral item is there, all at low-probability (LP) locations, all nine areas pooled. b, Target minus neutral item per pathway (both averaged over HP and LP locations). c, Distractor minus neutral item per pathway (both at LP locations). Stars in b, c: the mean over the window against 0, Wilcoxon signed-rank test, Benjamini-Hochberg over the three pathways per panel; b: early +0.13% signal, dorsal +0.26%, occipitotemporal +0.24%; c: early +0.07%, dorsal +0.15%, occipitotemporal +0.16%; all p < .001. d, e, Where the item lies relative to the run's HP location (HP rotates between runs, so each location is labelled per run): HP (purple), next to HP (the two neighbouring LP locations, pooled; mid purple) or opposite HP (light purple; dashed line in d); deconvolved with item type × location type as twelve event types. d, Each location type minus the mean of the four locations, item types pooled, per pathway. e, Each location type relative to the mean of the four locations over the window, item types pooled, in % of each subject's mean response over the four locations and three item types in that pathway; mean ± within-subject SEM (dorsal pathway: n = 26 in b, c, two subjects lack dorsal voxels at some item location; n = 25 in d, e, where sub-30 is also left out because its mean dorsal response is not positive, so a percentage of it is undefined). Early areas, repeated-measures ANOVA location type (HP, next, opposite) × item type (target, distractor, neutral): location type F(2, 54) = 7.67, p = .001; location type × item type F(4, 108) = 0.92, p = .454, so the item types are pooled. Brackets: planned contrasts on the mean over item types, Wilcoxon signed-rank test, uncorrected, with their p values: HP vs next to HP and HP vs opposite in every pathway, next to HP vs opposite where p < .05 (* p < .05, ** p < .01); early: HP − opposite −21.6%, p = .005; next − opposite −14.9 ± 6.0%, p = .022; HP − next −6.7 ± 3.6%, p = .095; all areas (right in e; location type F(2, 54) = 6.31, p = .003): HP − next −6.9 ± 2.7%, p = .018; HP − opposite −15.5%, p = .013; next − opposite −8.6 ± 4.6%, p = .060. Statistics per item type, for each pathway and all areas, HP against all LP locations, and with a DCT basis: Supplementary Table S (`tableS_item_locations_n28.md`). In d, e, a subject × pathway pair whose mean response over the twelve cells is not positive is left out (a percentage of it is undefined): sub-30, dorsal.
