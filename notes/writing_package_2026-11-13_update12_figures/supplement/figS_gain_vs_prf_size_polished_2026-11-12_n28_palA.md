**Figure S. Gain at the target and the distractor against pRF size.** Gain-field model fits with DoG pRFs, the pRFs refitted together with the gain fields. Median target and distractor gain per area (as in Fig. 5b; gains in %) against the area's pRF size (median over subjects of the voxel-weighted mean pRF σ at 1–2.5° eccentricity, log scale). Lines: slope per doubling of pRF size, the median of the per-subject OLS slopes over the nine areas, drawn through the centre of the points; target +27% per doubling (95% CI [+15%, +68%], 79% of participants positive, p = 0.0022, q = 0.0089); distractor +11% per doubling (95% CI [−11%, +32%], 61% of participants positive, p = 0.16, q = 0.31) (Wilcoxon signed-rank test of the per-subject slopes, Benjamini-Hochberg over the eight parameters tested). The slope may partly reflect a trade-off in the fits: within subjects (Spearman ρ over the nine areas, Wilcoxon across subjects), the target gain does not track the fitted weight of the items relative to the bar (median ρ = +0.04, p = 0.6) but falls with the width of the transient field (median ρ = −0.67, p = 7.5e−08), and the model-free enhancement at the item locations (Fig. 3) rises much less up the hierarchy.
