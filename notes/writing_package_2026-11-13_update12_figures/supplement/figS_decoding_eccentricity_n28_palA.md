**Figure S. The decoded difference at HP does not grow toward the items (Fig. 6, by eccentricity).** Decoding as in Fig. 6 (V1–V3 pooled, unconstrained decoder). Per subject and area, the decoded intensity on the bar's pixels in the quadrant facing HP minus that in the three other quadrants, in % of the latter, per ring of 0.25° eccentricity, mean of V1–V3 per subject; points and bars, mean ± SEM over subjects (n = 28); line and band, the gain-field model's prediction (as in Fig. 6; whole aperture −1.2 ± 0.5%), in the three bands shaded. Items (radius 0.75°) begin at 3.25° eccentricity, just outside the aperture, and their responses are in the BOLD data but not in the encoding model; if item responses leaking into the aperture produced the difference, it would grow toward the aperture edge. It does not: per band (one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test, uncorrected, * p < .05, ** p < .01) 0–1.5° −0.7 ± 0.6%, p = 0.069; 1.5–2.5° −1.6 ± 0.7%, p = 0.010; 2.5°+ −1.5 ± 0.7%, p = 0.020; whole aperture −1.3 ± 0.5%, p = 0.010; the three bands do not differ (Friedman test, p = 0.51; outer minus inner band, two-sided Wilcoxon p = 0.37). Within 0.75° of the fovea there is no difference (+0.2 ± 0.5%).
