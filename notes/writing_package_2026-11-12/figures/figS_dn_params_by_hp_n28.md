**Figure S. Divisive-normalization (DN) pRF parameters fitted separately per HP condition (exploratory).** One DN pRF fit per subject, ROI and HP condition (the runs in which HP was at one of the four locations; HRF fixed per subject × ROI; no cross-validation). Voxels: positive pRF amplitude in the all-runs fit and in all four condition fits, all-runs pRF centre inside the bar aperture (3.17°) and at least 0.25° from both meridians, so that its quadrant is unambiguous. For each voxel and HP condition, the voxel's quadrant (from the all-runs fit) is the HP quadrant, a quadrant next to it (two conditions, averaged) or the opposite quadrant. Each parameter is expressed within voxel (log ratio to the voxel's geometric mean over the four conditions; difference from its mean for the BOLD baseline and R²), then the median over voxels per subject × ROI × relation (subject × ROI pairs with at least 10 voxels; n = 28 in V1–V3; the pilots sub-01 and sub-02 never entered this analysis, which needs all four HP conditions). Points and bars: mean over subjects and SEM. Tests per ROI and parameter: Wilcoxon signed-rank HP vs opposite quadrant and Friedman over the three relations, Benjamini-Hochberg across ROIs within each parameter. In V1–V3, pRFs in the HP quadrant are slightly larger (σ, HP − opposite log ratio +0.011 to +0.018; q = .045–.051), have a lower peak gain (−0.010 to −0.017; q = .047–.066) and a stronger surround (+0.028 to +0.036; q = .010–.101) than in the opposite quadrant; the neural and surround baselines (b, d) and their ratio do not differ (all q ≥ .11 in V1–V3; LO b q = .084). Friedman tests across the three relations are mostly not significant. Exploratory: per-condition fits are not part of the planned analyses, have no held-out test, and are reported for completeness. Source: `notes/data/dn_by_hp_stats_2026-11.tsv` (identical when rerun with the pilots excluded, `notes/data/n28/dn_by_hp_stats_2026-11.tsv`).
