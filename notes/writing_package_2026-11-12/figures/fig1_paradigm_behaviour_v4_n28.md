**Figure 1. Paradigm and behaviour.** a, Search display with the bar in its 3.17° aperture: 8 items on a ring at 4°, with target and distractor at the four diagonal positions; percentages are over all trials. The target has the odd orientation (participants report the dot on it) and never appears at the distractor's location; there is no distractor on 20% of trials. b, Two trials to scale, with the bar position every 2 s below; the bar sweeps the aperture in four directions (rightward, leftward, downward, upward; 42 s each with 4.8 s rest, the sequence twice per run), and the HP location across blocks of runs (example order). c, Response time with the distractor at the HP location, at an LP location, or absent. d, Response time with the target at the HP or an LP location, on trials with a distractor. e, Intertrial priming as within-subject differences: response time when the target (orange) or the distractor (blue) appears at the same location as on the previous trial minus when it appears at another location; distractor repeats are compared within HP and within LP trials and averaged. Target repeat − switch −38.7 ms [−48.8, −28.4], 26/28, p = 3.7e−08; distractor repeat − switch −15.1 ms [−29.2, −6.2], 20/28, p = 0.0013 (median [bootstrapped 95% CI], subjects on the side of the median, Wilcoxon signed-rank). The raw response times of these conditions are in Supplementary Fig. figS_priming_rt_v4_n28. Icons below each condition: the four possible locations, with HP ringed in red where the condition is defined by it (c, d); filled, the item on this trial, hollow, on the previous trial (e: repeat minus switch display). c-e, n = 28 (the two pilot participants, sub-01 and sub-02, with a faster bar and a different HP schedule, are left out), correct trials. Above the brackets in c, d and above each strip in e: the median within-subject difference and the Wilcoxon signed-rank test (** p < .01, *** p < .001); lower rows (c, d) and e: the within-subject differences, every strip and e on the same ms scale. c, d, lower rows: distractor HP − LP −25.0 ms [−32.4, −20.0], 28/28, p = 7.5e−09; LP − absent +42.6 ms [+27.0, +50.7], 26/28, p = 1e−07; target HP − LP +24.3 ms [+18.5, +36.4], 24/28, p = 1.5e−06. In c and d (upper rows), dots are subjects, triangles subjects beyond the axis limits, and large points and bars the mean ± within-subject SEM (Cousineau-Morey). In the difference strips and e, dots are subjects (beyond the axis limits drawn at the edge), diamonds the median and bars its bootstrapped 95% CI (10000 resamples).
