**Figure S. Width of the gain fields.** Full width at half maximum (FWHM = 2.355 σ) per subject and area of the sustained field (red) and of the transient field shared by the target and the distractor gain (grey), from the joint gain-field fits with DoG pRFs (m7, final fits; n = 28; log axis). Dots are subjects; points and bars, the median and its bootstrapped 95% CI over all fits, including those at a bound. Numbers above and below a series: fits at the upper (σ = 8°, FWHM 18.8°) or lower (σ = 0.5°, FWHM 1.2°) bound of σ, not drawn as dots. Dotted line: the spacing of neighbouring items (5.7°). Sustained: 12% of all fits at a bound (per area V1 29%, V2 14%, V3 7%, V3AB 7%, IPS 12%, hV4 14%, VO 10%, LO 11%, TO 7%); median FWHM [interquartile range over subjects] V1 5.2° [3.3–18.8]; V2 4.7° [3.4–7.3]; V3 4.6° [2.7–7.2]; V3AB 3.2° [2.6–3.9]; IPS 3.1° [2.6–6.0]; hV4 3.0° [2.6–9.3]; VO 2.6° [2.4–3.3]; LO 3.2° [2.7–6.2]; TO 2.5° [2.3–2.8]. Transient: often at a bound (per area V1 43%, V2 50%, V3 54%, V3AB 59%, IPS 52%, hV4 68%, VO 33%, LO 46%, TO 54%; IPS mostly at the upper bound), so its width is poorly constrained and trades off with the transient gain amplitude; median FWHM [IQR] V1 9.5° [6.5–18.8]; V2 9.3° [3.9–18.8]; V3 5.5° [1.2–14.0]; V3AB 4.4° [1.2–7.7]; IPS 18.3° [7.3–18.8]; hV4 1.2° [1.2–4.7]; VO 5.0° [1.2–8.9]; LO 4.8° [1.7–16.6]; TO 1.7° [1.2–4.6]. The sustained width is the well-determined one. At half maximum the sustained field (median FWHM above) stays within the spacing of neighbouring items in most ROIs (early areas ≈ 5°, others ≈ 3°).
