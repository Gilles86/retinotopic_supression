**Supplementary Figure S. Widths of the fitted gain fields, group level.** Zero-sum main model (DoG pRFs, pRFs and gain fields fitted jointly), n = 28. For each participant and area, every parameter was held at its fit except the width of one field, which was set to each of 17 values (FWHM 1.2–18.8°), and that field's gains were re-solved by least squares: the sustained gain along the zero-sum direction (HP = ¾ dS, LP = −¼ dS), and the target and distractor gains (distractor equal at HP and LP, as in the model). Colour: the training error above its minimum, summed over participants, scaled per area (white, best width). Dot: the group minimum; bar: the range of minima when leaving out one participant at a time. (a) Sustained field. (b) Transient target and distractor field; at 18.8° it is nearly uniform over the display, at 1.2° it acts only at the item locations.
