Gain-field model: per subject and run, the BOLD of V1–V3 was simulated from the subject's joint fit (pRFs fitted together with the gain fields on all runs: sustained HP and LP gains and field width, transient distractor and target gains, search items, one HRF per subject and area) plus the real run's residuals under that fit, phase-randomised (one random phase per frequency for all voxels), and decoded exactly as the data, on the decoder's voxels that are also in the joint fit; a paired simulation with the five gains at 0 and the same noise was decoded alike, and the prediction is the difference per subject, averaged like the data.
