**Figure S. Width of the gain fields among fits with a reliable gain, and how well each width is constrained.** Joint gain-field fits with DoG pRFs and one HRF per participant and area (final fits; n = 28 participants, 241 participant × area fits). For each fit, the gains of one field were re-estimated by least squares with every other parameter (pRFs, HRF, item scaling, the other field) held at the joint fit, run by run, giving a leave-one-run-out jackknife SE for each gain (calibrated: half the odd − even-run difference over the SE has median 0.70, 0.66 and 0.56 for the sustained gain at HP, at the other locations and the target gain; 0.67 expected). A gain counts as reliable when |gain| / SE > 2. a, Full width at half maximum (FWHM = 2.355 σ) of the sustained field per area: all fits (pale) and fits whose sustained gain is reliable at HP or at the other three locations (solid). Points and bars, median and bootstrapped 95% CI; small dots, fits (values beyond 1–20° drawn at the edge); numbers, fits per series. Dotted line, the 5.7° between neighbouring possible item locations. Among fits with a reliable sustained gain, the median FWHM is 2.7° [2.6, 2.9] (participant medians over areas; 25 of 28 below 5.7°, Wilcoxon against 5.7°, p < 10⁻⁴), below 5.7° in V1 (n = 8, q = .018), V3AB, IPS, VO, LO and TO (q ≤ .03), not in V2 (n = 6), V3 (n = 12) or hV4 (n = 15) (Benjamini-Hochberg across nine areas). b, The same for the transient field, kept when the target gain is reliable. Even then the transient width sits at a bound of the fit (0.5° or 8°) in 58% of fits, so it is not interpreted. c, d, Width of each fit's 95% interval for the field width (FWHM degrees) against |gain| / SE. The interval comes from the profile of the fit error over the width (σ on a log grid from 0.5° to 8°; at each width the field's gains re-solved, all other parameters fixed at the joint fit) and the leave-one-run-out jackknife of the profile minimum, because the residuals are correlated over time and voxels and a χ² threshold with the nominal residual variance would be far too narrow. Triangles: profile minimum at the grid edge, so the interval is open. The sustained width is pinned down only when its gain is clearly non-zero (median interval 17.7° at |gain| / SE < 1, 1.9° at > 3; Spearman ρ = −.74); the transient width stays poorly constrained even for reliable target gains. Source: `notes/data/field_widths_reliable_n28_2026-11-12_*.tsv`; code `retsupp/modeling/fit_width_profiles.py`, `retsupp/visualize/paper/field_widths_reliable.py`. These are on the current joint fits with each voxel's pRF held at the fit without gain fields; a refit of pRFs and gain fields together is running and may shift them slightly.
