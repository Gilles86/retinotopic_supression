# Update 7 — field widths conditional on a reliable gain (applies on top of update 6)

What changes in the manuscript:
1. **Width paragraph (Results).** Cite widths only from fits with a reliable gain. Claim (a), narrower than the 5.7°
   between neighbouring item locations, holds: 2.7° [2.6, 2.9], 25 of 28 participants, p < 10⁻⁴. Replace the 3.2° and
   the per-pathway medians. Suggested wording: "Where its gain was reliably different from zero (|gain| > 2 SE; 6–22
   participants per area), the sustained field had a full width at half maximum of 2.7° [2.6, 2.9] (median over
   participants), narrower than the 5.7° between neighbouring item locations in 25 of 28 participants (p < 10⁻⁴). Its
   width was well determined only where its gain was reliable: the width's 95% interval narrowed from 18° at |gain| < 1
   SE to 1.9° at > 3 SE. The transient width was not constrained even for reliable target gains (at a bound of the fit
   in 58% of them) and is not interpreted."
2. **Claim (b), wider in V1–V3 than in higher areas: does not survive** (3.4° vs 2.5°, 13 of 20, p = .053; no difference
   at |gain| > 3 SE). The earlier 4.6–5.2° early medians came from fits with a near-zero sustained gain. Remove the
   early-vs-late width contrast and the Discussion link to Richter et al.'s "broad in early visual cortex, refined
   later", or state: "We found no evidence that the sustained field is broader in early than in later visual areas once
   its width was estimated only where its gain was reliable."
3. **Methods sentence.** "Each gain's standard error was estimated by a leave-one-run-out jackknife of the gain
   re-estimated by least squares with all other parameters fixed at the joint fit; widths are reported for fits whose
   gain exceeded twice its standard error, and each width's 95% interval from the jackknife of the profile minimum over
   the width."
4. **Fig. 5a maps:** use the restricted sustained widths (V3 2.7° instead of 4.6°; hV4 2.6°; TO 2.5°) — a re-render will
   follow with the Fig. 5 v11 after the joint pRF + gain refit; until then the current v10 stays.
5. **Supplementary numbering:** see the numbering table (S3 per-area item locations, S4 field widths, S13 baseline vs
   gain; later figures shift).

Caveat: these are on the current joint fits (pRFs held at the fit without gain fields); the joint pRF + gain refit may
shift them slightly.
