**Figure 5. The gain fields: gain at the target and the distractor, and less sustained gain at the high-probability (HP) location.** Gain-field model fits with DoG pRFs (n = 28); gains are in % (the change in drive at the centre of a field: +30%, 30% more); differences between gains (HP − LP) are in percentage points of gain, written as %. a, The fitted parametric gain field over the search display, not data: for each area, the median over participants (n = 28) of each participant's fitted parameters, put through the model's field equations. The display is rotated so that the HP location is at the top (red dashed ring); small circles, the eight item positions on the 4° ring (dotted; stronger: the four possible target and distractor locations); grey circle, the 3.17° aperture of the mapping bar; +, fixation. Left three maps, the sustained field at HP minus at the low−probability (LP) locations: only the HP−specific part of the sustained field, the area's median HP − LP difference estimated alone (dS, the value panel c plots, printed in bold next to HP) times the field's Gaussian at HP (the area's median fitted width); thin red line, the half−maximum (FWHM) contour of that field. One scale for the three areas, white (no difference) to dark red at the deepest area's HP − LP (−3.0%; no headroom, so that area saturates). The sustained field itself is a suppression at all four possible locations in most areas (7 of 9: V3, V3AB, IPS, hV4, VO, LO, TO); the grey lines under each map give the sustained gain at LP and at HP (median joint-fit LP gain; at HP, that plus the HP − LP difference drawn). Right three maps, the transient fields of the target (orange, around the orange diamond, at an LP location, here opposite HP) and of the distractor (blue, around the blue dot, drawn at an LP location next to HP) in one map; values, the target gain of panel b and the distractor gain averaged over its locations, as in panel b (the transient HP − LP difference is in panel d); both on one scale, 0 to the largest value, so their colours compare directly. The transient fields exist only while a search display is on; their fitted width is often at a bound of the fit (0.5° or 8°), so it is set for display to the same FWHM of 3.2° in every area and is not interpreted (field widths per participant: Supplementary Fig., field widths). Values used: V3: sustained gain at LP −0.4%, at HP −3.5% (HP − LP = dS estimated alone −3.0%, the median of panel c; joint-fit dS −9.0%), width σ 1.94° (FWHM 4.6°); target gain +30%; distractor gain +3%; hV4: sustained gain at LP −5.8%, at HP −7.3% (HP − LP = dS estimated alone −1.5%, the median of panel c; joint-fit dS −5.7%), width σ 1.29° (FWHM 3.0°); target gain +56%; distractor gain +22%; TO: sustained gain at LP −35%, at HP −37% (HP − LP = dS estimated alone −1.9%, the median of panel c; joint-fit dS −2.4%), width σ 1.07° (FWHM 2.5°); target gain +22%; distractor gain +8%. V3, hV4 and TO are the areas where the sustained gain is reliably lower at HP than at LP in the joint fit (q < .05); estimated alone, as drawn, the difference is reliable in V3 and TO (panel c). The sum of the three fields during a search display is in Supplementary Fig. (gain field during search). b–d: dots are subjects (triangles, beyond the axis), points and bars the median and bootstrapped 95% CI; stars, Wilcoxon signed-rank test against 0, Benjamini-Hochberg across the nine ROIs, * q < .05, ** q < .01, *** q < .001 (exact p and q per ROI in the parameter table). b, Gain at the target (orange) and at the distractor (blue, averaged over its locations) while the search display is on, all terms fitted jointly. The gain against pRF size is in a supplementary figure. c, dS = g_HP − g_LP (in percentage points), the sustained gain at the HP location minus that at the low-probability (LP) locations, each term estimated alone on the item model with sustained gain (conditional estimate). d, The gain analogue of Fig. 1c, d: dD = g_HP_dyn − g_LP_dyn, the gain the distractor draws at the HP location minus at an LP location (blue), next to dT, the target gain at the HP location minus at an LP location (orange), each estimated alone as in c; the display icons are those of Fig. 1 (HP ringed in red; the item at HP minus at an LP location).
